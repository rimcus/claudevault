# DoubleSpeed AI

*Tool for creating TikTok accounts in the cloud.*

## Background

Named in [[sources/codyschneider-ai-media-company-playbook]] as the tool used to create TikTok accounts remotely/in the cloud, rather than provisioning and managing physical devices. No further detail on the tool's mechanics is given in the source.

## Role in This Wiki

Step 1 of [[concepts/tiktok-reel-farm|Cody Schneider's TikTok reel farm tactic]] — provisions the account cohort that [[entities/nano-banana|Nano Banana]]-generated slideshows are then posted to.

## Key Contributions / Actions

- Creates the 10-account cohort described in [[sources/codyschneider-ai-media-company-playbook]] producing ~300K aggregate impressions.
