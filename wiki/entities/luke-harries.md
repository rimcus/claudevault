# Luke Harries

*Head of Growth at [[entities/elevenlabs|ElevenLabs]], where he leads marketing, product, engineering, and developer experience for growth; prior roles at PostHog (transcribed "Post Hoc"/"Postdog" [low confidence on exact name]) and Microsoft; active angel investor.*

## Background

Met [[entities/mati-staniszewski|Mati Staniszewski]] (later ElevenLabs co-founder) at a Cambridge hackathon at 19; the same hackathon connects to Wordware's founder (largest-ever YC raise, ~$40M). Founded a startup called Fella (with co-founder Richie) that went through YC, pivoted through non-profit COVID drive-through testing in San Francisco during the pandemic, then into a GLP-1/weight-loss digital CBT clinic that reached ~$300K revenue before stalling; Harries left to join PostHog, and Richie continued the company past $30M/year in revenue after the Ozempic wave hit. Joined ElevenLabs as an early growth hire when the company had ~1 million users and three junior, generalist growth staff.

## Role in This Wiki

The first source in this wiki addressing general growth marketing (launches, video, org design, founder brand, CAC/payback framing) rather than B2B cold-email/outbound GTM tooling. His practitioner voice is dense with falsifiable, opinionated claims — the wiki treats him as a primary voice on growth-team structure and launch execution, distinct from [[entities/cody-schneider|Cody Schneider]]'s automation/agent-focused voice and [[entities/alex-vacca|Alex Vacca]]'s outbound-signal-focused voice already in the wiki.

## Key Contributions / Actions

- Built ElevenLabs' growth function from 3 junior generalists into a sharded, multi-team org (see [[concepts/sharded-growth-teams]]).
- Codified ElevenLabs' tiered [[concepts/launch-playbook]] (200K–700K views per major launch).
- Angel investor in [[entities/lovable|Lovable]], Runna (sold to Strava — his first exit), and Captions.
- Passed on angel-investing in ElevenLabs pre-launch, later acknowledged as a costly miss.
