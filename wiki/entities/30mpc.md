# 30 Minutes to President's Club (30MPC)

*Sales media and training company/podcast co-founded by [[entities/armand-farrokh|Armand Farrokh]], built around short, tactical, no-filler sales content — contrasted in-source with long-form podcast formats the way [[concepts/counter-positioning]] describes elsewhere in this wiki (Farrokh explicitly frames 30MPC's short/concise format as its defining edge).*

## Background

Co-hosted by Farrokh and Mark Kosoglow, referred to in the transcript as "Mark Koslow" [medium-high confidence this refers to Kosoglow, the former Outreach CRO] — credited in this source with the "connect the dots" ranked-trigger-template personalization method (see [[concepts/outbound-targeting-triggers]]). Farrokh sent a 30MPC podcast episode as pre-interview prep material to SDR candidates at Pave, using it as the "basic framework" candidates were expected to apply during the mock-cold-call interview round.

## Role in This Wiki

Introduces two named contributors — Farrokh and Kosoglow — and a distinct sales-media brand into the wiki's outbound/SDR domain layer. Functions as a media-brand entity comparable to how [[entities/spicy-lemon|Spicy Lemon]] (producer of the Squeeze and Scale podcast) sits in the wiki's cold-email domain.

## Key Contributions / Actions

- Publishes a free newsletter (30mpc.com/newsletter) covering weekly actionable sales tactics.
- Podcast content used directly as SDR-candidate training material by Farrokh at Pave.
- Co-host Mark Kosoglow credited with the ranked trigger-template personalization framework.
