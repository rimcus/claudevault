# iBanFirst

*Fintech company; [[entities/maxime-champoux|Maxime Champoux]]'s first employer, where he was the first hire and built the company's first Core Banking system from scratch.*

## Background

Champoux joined at a time (~15 years before this recording) when "startup" wasn't yet a fashionable career path in France. As first employee, his initial project was building a Core Banking system with no accessible playbook to reference — a 2.5-year build. He went on to specialize in payments APIs (pre-DSP2/open banking) and later shipped what he describes as the first fully-online B2B account-opening flow in the category: a B2C-style onboarding experience scaled to a legal entity with multiple shareholders, including a compliance-timing detail (issuing the account's IBAN immediately post-onboarding, ahead of full KYC/KYB completion, since the actual compliance risk is unauthorized payment rather than merely holding an account number).

## Role in This Wiki

The origin point of the Core Banking and onboarding expertise Champoux later brought to [[entities/qonto|Qonto]] (rebuilding the same system there in 6 months instead of 2.5 years) — the wiki's clearest example of domain expertise directly compressing a second build's timeline.

## Key Contributions / Actions

- Built the company's first Core Banking system (2.5 years, from scratch, no established playbook).
- Shipped an early fully-online B2B account-opening flow with a specific KYC/KYB-timing compliance approach.
- Its onboarding flow was later inherited and rebuilt upon by Qonto when Qonto launched.
