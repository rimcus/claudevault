# Mati Staniszewski

*Co-founder/CEO of [[entities/elevenlabs|ElevenLabs]]; friend of [[entities/luke-harries|Luke Harries]] since a Cambridge hackathon at age 19.*

## Background

Pitched Harries on the ElevenLabs plan roughly a year and a half before the primary source's recording, while swimming in Hampstead Heath ponds — the company name reportedly comes from that conversation (the water was 11°C). The original plan as pitched: build the world's best audio AI models first, then sell that capability broadly across consumer, creator, developer, and enterprise use cases — a plan Harries considered a "terrible go-to-market plan" at the time and passed on investing in. Six months after that conversation, Staniszewski called Harries back to report ElevenLabs had hit 1 million users and needed someone to lead growth.

## Role in This Wiki

Named as the source of ElevenLabs' ambition to build "a bigger company than Google" and its resulting willingness to run multiple simultaneous, non-compounding product lines — the origin of the [[concepts/sharded-growth-teams]] structure the wiki documents. Also the central example in [[concepts/founder-brand-strategy]]: a founder who is genuinely strong at one channel format (in-person speaking/fireside chats) and is deliberately not pushed onto channels (X/Twitter) he doesn't naturally engage with.

## Key Contributions / Actions

- Co-founded ElevenLabs on the horizontal "best model first, sell to everyone" thesis.
- Set the company's stated ambition ("bigger than Google") that Harries cites as the reason ElevenLabs keeps expanding into disconnected product lines (reader app, creative platform, enterprise conversational AI) rather than staying narrowly focused.
- Personal growth-channel strength: large in-person speaking events and fireside chats — not organic X/Twitter activity.
