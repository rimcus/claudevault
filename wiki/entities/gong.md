# Gong

A revenue-intelligence platform that records and transcribes sales calls.

## Role in This Wiki

Named by [[entities/cody-schneider|Cody Schneider]] in [[sources/codyschneider-two-agents-podcast]] as one of the internal sources the [[concepts/organic-content-agent-loop|organic content agent]] mines for raw material — alongside interviews, Slack, and Notion. Gong's specific contribution is recorded, transcribed sales-call language: the same kind of unprompted, real-customer-language source the wiki already tracks as valuable for copy and persona work (see [[concepts/icp-avatar]]'s "quote rule" and [[sources/codyschneider-transcript-personas]]'s transcript mining), here repurposed as raw material for LinkedIn content instead of ad copy or ICP definitions.
