# Veo3

Google's AI video generation model. Listed alongside [[HeyGen]] and [[Seedance]] as an option for producing UGC-style ad videos from Claude-written scripts in [[Cody Schneider]]'s Facebook ads pipeline.

## Role in This Wiki

One of three AI video generation options in the [[AI UGC Ads]] pipeline. Takes a script as input and produces a video ad without human creators.

## Position in Stack

```
Reddit quotes → Claude (30-second UGC scripts) → Veo3 (video) → Facebook Ads
```
