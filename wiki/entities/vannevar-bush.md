# Vannevar Bush

American engineer and science administrator (1890–1974). Wrote "As We May Think" (1945), which introduced the concept of the [[Memex]] — a personal, curated knowledge store with associative trails between documents. Widely cited as a conceptual predecessor to hypertext and personal computing.

## Role in This Wiki

Referenced by [[Andrej Karpathy]] as the historical precedent for the [[LLM-Maintained Wiki]] pattern. Karpathy argues the Memex vision was closer to the LLM wiki idea than to what the open web became.

## Key Contributions

- Proposed the Memex in "As We May Think" (Atlantic, 1945)
- Memex was private, actively curated, with associative trails as valuable as the documents themselves
- The unsolved problem of the Memex was: *who does the maintenance?* — LLMs now answer this
