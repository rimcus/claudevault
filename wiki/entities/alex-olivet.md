# Alex Olivet

*Host of SaaS Connection (French-language SaaS podcast); founder of Collect, a client-onboarding platform for SaaS companies.*

## Background

Founded Collect, which integrates into client workflow tools to streamline onboarding specifically for SaaS companies handling high volumes of new customers, with AI built in to accelerate the onboarding step. Worked in growth ("growth hacking") at an early fintech (heard in the transcript as "Meljet"/"Medjet" [low confidence on the exact name]) roughly 8 years before this recording, which is where he first met [[entities/maxime-champoux|Maxime Champoux]] (then a PM on the API team there).

## Role in This Wiki

Interviewer/host for the wiki's first source on AI-native engineering process. Functions in this wiki primarily as the interviewer drawing out [[entities/maxime-champoux|Champoux]]'s account rather than as an independent source of claims himself — parallel to [[entities/harry-stebbings|Harry Stebbings]]'s role in the ElevenLabs source, though with less independent corroborating content in this transcript specifically.

## Key Contributions / Actions

- Hosts SaaS Connection, interviewing francophone SaaS founders on growth, marketing, sales, product, and retention practices.
- Founded and runs Collect, an AI-assisted client-onboarding platform for SaaS companies.
