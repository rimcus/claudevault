# Ordinal

A LinkedIn content scheduling tool that posts across multiple accounts and streams performance analytics back to the posting agent.

## Role in This Wiki

The scheduling and feedback layer in [[entities/cody-schneider|Cody Schneider]]'s [[concepts/organic-content-agent-loop|organic content agent loop]] ([[sources/codyschneider-two-agents-podcast]]). Ordinal handles two jobs at once: cross-account distribution (personal profiles and/or topic-based pages), and closing the loop by feeding analytics back into the agent so it can decide what to snowball or remix.

## Pipeline Position

Sourced/drafted content → **Ordinal** (schedule across accounts) → analytics stream → agent decision (snowball / remix / repost at 90-day intervals)
