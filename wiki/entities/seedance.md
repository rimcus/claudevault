# Seedance

An AI video generation tool. Listed alongside [[HeyGen]] and [[Veo3]] as an option for producing UGC-style ad videos from Claude-written scripts in [[Cody Schneider]]'s Facebook ads pipeline.

## Role in This Wiki

One of three AI video generation options in the [[AI UGC Ads]] pipeline. Takes a script as input and produces a video ad without human creators.

## Position in Stack

```
Reddit quotes → Claude (30-second UGC scripts) → Seedance (video) → Facebook Ads
```
