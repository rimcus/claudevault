# Pave

*Compensation-benchmarking startup; [[entities/armand-farrokh|Armand Farrokh]] joined as employee #9 and Head of Sales, taking the company from $0 to $13M ARR in two years before becoming VP of Sales.*

## Background

Farrokh joined with effectively zero internal HR-buyer network (Pave's actual customer base) but strong access to the company's CEO and investor relationships, which he systematized into a referral pipeline (see [[concepts/executive-referral-pipeline]]). The company beat its own year-one $4M ARR target by hitting $6M, and reached $13M ARR within two years overall — genuinely strong growth. However, Pave had set a $20M target during the 2021 ZIRP-era funding environment; when the market corrected in 2022, leadership held onto that now-unrealistic target rather than resetting it to something achievable (Farrokh suggests ~$12M would have been realistic) and right-sizing headcount to match — leaving the team feeling like failures every week despite having genuinely doubled revenue in a down market.

## Role in This Wiki

The source of the wiki's clearest example of a target-recalibration failure: a case where the underlying performance was strong but an unadjusted, bubble-era target made the team feel like it was failing regardless. Also the site of Farrokh's top-ever SDR hire (Alan Chen) and the "Exec Shark Tank Week" executive-referral program.

## Key Contributions / Actions

- Site of the "Exec Shark Tank Week" program: mapping executives' first-degree LinkedIn networks and having SDRs ghostwrite intro messages for executives to send (see [[concepts/executive-referral-pipeline]]).
- Site of the VC portfolio-operations referral program (Andreessen Horowitz, Index, Y Combinator) generating 5-10 intros/week.
- Hired Alan Chen, Farrokh's top-ever SDR, via an unsolicited but expertly-constructed cold-email Loom video (see [[concepts/sdr-hiring-signal]]).
- Grew from $0 to $13M ARR in two years under Farrokh's sales leadership, beating its own year-one target.
