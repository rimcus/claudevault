# Teamfluence

A LinkedIn profile viewer tracking tool. Identifies who is checking your LinkedIn profile before they take any visible action — a high-intent signal that someone is evaluating you or your offer.

## Role in This Wiki

Part of [[SalesCaptain]]'s capture system — the layer that converts anonymous LinkedIn attention into known prospects. Profile views are a strong pre-intent signal: someone researching you before reaching out is a fundamentally different buyer than a cold lead.

## Position in the Capture System

| Tool | What it captures |
|------|-----------------|
| Teamfluence | Profile viewers |
| [[Trigify]] | Post engagers |
| [[RB2B]] | Website visitors from LinkedIn traffic |
