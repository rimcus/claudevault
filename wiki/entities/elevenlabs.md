# ElevenLabs

*AI audio company (text-to-speech, speech-to-text, sound effects, voice isolation, conversational AI agents); raised $281M at a $3.3B valuation as of the primary source's recording; co-founded by [[entities/mati-staniszewski|Mati Staniszewski]] and a co-founder referred to as "Peter"/"Piotr" (model lead).*

## Background

Founded on a horizontal thesis stated plainly by co-founder Mati at founding: step one, build the world's best audio AI models; step two, sell that capability to everyone — commuters, audiobook/voice-over creators, and developers building voice agents. [[entities/luke-harries|Luke Harries]], hearing this plan pre-launch, judged it a "terrible go-to-market plan" and passed on angel investing — a decision he later regretted. The company reached 1 million users before Harries joined as an early growth hire (team of three junior generalists at the time). At the point of ElevenLabs' initial breakout to roughly $1B valuation, Harries estimates the core audio model itself accounted for something like half the company's enterprise value.

## Role in This Wiki

The first company profiled in this wiki as a case study in **horizontal, multi-product growth strategy** — contrasted explicitly against the single-ICP compounding model (PostHog) that is otherwise the wiki's default GTM assumption. Also the source of the wiki's first documented **no-PM, product-engineer-owned roadmap** organizational model, and its first **sharded growth team** structure.

## Key Contributions / Actions

- Sells one horizontal capability (best-in-class audio AI) across four distinct product lines: developer API, consumer reader app, creative platform (text-to-audiobook/voiceover), and enterprise conversational-AI agents.
- Runs [[concepts/sharded-growth-teams]]: horizontal channel-specialist team + per-product growth-lead pods (enterprise marketing scaling to ~20 people; mobile app growth team at 5–10).
- Runs a [[concepts/launch-playbook]] producing 200K–700K views per major (Tier 1) launch.
- No PM function — see [[concepts/product-engineers-no-pms]]; ~60–70% of core engineering code is AI-generated.
- Affiliate channel, stood up by one engineer in a week ~1.5 years before the interview, grew unattended to "tens of thousands of dollars" MRR/month — cited by Harries as the clearest example of the cost of under-staffing a proven channel.
- Explicit strategic boundary: builds end-to-end conversational-AI-agent infrastructure but deliberately does not build full digital-avatar platforms, to remain a trusted infrastructure partner to companies like Synthesia and Captions rather than competing with them.
- Homepage built largely around free-to-try, no-login mini-tools per product line (see [[concepts/seo-mini-tools]]).
- Its TTS product is used as a third-party production tool outside the company itself: [[entities/cody-schneider|Cody Schneider]] narrates agent-written podcast scripts with ElevenLabs as part of his [[concepts/podcast-newsletter-growth-loop|podcast + newsletter growth loop]] ([[sources/codyschneider-ai-media-company-playbook]]).
