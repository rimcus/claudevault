# Carta

*Fintech company providing cap-table and stock-option management software; [[entities/armand-farrokh|Armand Farrokh]]'s employer as a mid-market AE and then Director of SDR — the site of his SDR-turnaround, targeting, culture-building, and VC-focused ABM stories.*

## Background

Was mid-transition from inbound to outbound when Farrokh joined as a mid-market AE: sales team size had doubled but lead flow stayed flat, leaving average quota attainment on his 8-person pod at 40-50% (only one rep hitting quota). Farrokh became the #1 rep company-wide within 6-9 months at 250% of quota using a repeatable cold-call opener and problem-first pitch, then was promoted directly into SDR leadership — inheriting a 20-person team split across San Francisco and Salt Lake City right after its top performers had been promoted out.

## Role in This Wiki

The setting for the wiki's most detailed account yet of an SDR org turnaround, a redesigned SDR interview process, daily culture-building tactics, firmographic-plus-trigger account targeting, and a coordinated marketing/SDR/physical-mail ABM play targeting venture capital funds.

## Key Contributions / Actions

- Site of the "Flip 2, Fire 2, Hire 2" SDR turnaround (see [[concepts/sdr-org-turnaround-playbook]]).
- Site of the daily "dial blitz" culture practice and the company-wide "Show and Tell" campaign elevating SDR status (see [[concepts/sdr-culture-building]]).
- Source of the "stale cap table" targeting example — a company can fit every firmographic ICP filter and still be a bad target without an active trigger event (see [[concepts/outbound-targeting-triggers]]).
- Ran a coordinated ABM play against VC funds combining SDR outreach, marketing display ads, and physical-goods mailers (literal pies) — see [[concepts/abm-account-based-pipeline-plays]].
