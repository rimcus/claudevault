# Andrej Karpathy

AI researcher and educator. Former Director of AI at Tesla, founding member of OpenAI. Known for making deep learning accessible through clear writing and open-source projects (micrograd, nanoGPT, llm.c).

## Role in This Wiki

Originator of the [[LLM-Maintained Wiki]] pattern that this vault is built on. His gist describing the pattern is the founding source of this knowledge base.

## Key Contributions / Actions

- Authored the LLM Wiki pattern (the founding source of this vault)
- Created nanoGPT, micrograd, llm.c — educational implementations of LLMs
- Former Director of AI at Tesla (Autopilot)
- Founding member of OpenAI
