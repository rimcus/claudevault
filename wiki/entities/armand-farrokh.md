# Armand Farrokh

*Co-founder of [[entities/30mpc|30 Minutes to President's Club (30MPC)]]; former Head of Sales/VP of Sales at [[entities/pave|Pave]] ($0→$13M ARR in two years); former Director of SDR and mid-market AE at [[entities/carta|Carta]].*

## Background

Started in tech-adjacent sales at Northwestern Mutual selling insurance, a top-2%-nationally performer as a college intern — until a $20,000 clawback (a structural feature of insurance-sales compensation) made him quit sales for 2-3 years to work in management consulting (PwC, then an MBB-staffed strategy house). Returned to sales at Carta as a mid-market AE, skipping the SDR step on the strength of his insurance and finance background, during a period when Carta had doubled its sales team size while transitioning from inbound to outbound without a corresponding lead-flow increase (average quota attainment on his 8-person pod was 40-50%). Built a repeatable cold-call opener and problem-first pitch, became the #1 rep company-wide within 6-9 months at 250% of quota, and was promoted directly from AE to Director of SDR/manager of managers when the standing SDR leader was let go — skipping the frontline-manager step entirely. Tripled outbound pipeline while doubling team size at Carta, then moved to Pave as Head of Sales, taking the company from $0 to $13M ARR in two years and becoming VP of Sales.

## Role in This Wiki

The wiki's first practitioner voice on **human SDR organization-building** — hiring, interviewing, daily culture, targeting discipline, and creative pipeline generation — largely independent of AI tooling, making his account a useful pre-AI-era baseline against the wiki's other, more AI-native sources.

## Key Contributions / Actions

- Built and ran [[concepts/sdr-org-turnaround-playbook]] ("Flip 2, Fire 2, Hire 2") at Carta, tripling outbound pipeline while doubling team size.
- Redesigned the SDR interview process into a 3-round funnel (see [[concepts/sdr-hiring-signal]]), finding that none of his top 5 SDR hires ever had prior SDR experience.
- Built [[concepts/sdr-culture-building]]: the daily "dial blitz" and public-recognition system, plus a company-wide "Show and Tell" campaign to elevate SDR status org-wide.
- Developed [[concepts/outbound-targeting-triggers]]: firmographic-plus-trigger-event account targeting, "shuttle run" account-research training, and ranked trigger-template personalization.
- Ran [[concepts/abm-account-based-pipeline-plays]] (VC-fund ABM at Carta) and [[concepts/executive-referral-pipeline]] (VC portfolio-ops referrals and internal "Exec Shark Tank Week" at Pave).
- Documented [[concepts/event-pipeline-pre-booking]]: pre-booking meetings ahead of major industry events, plus "floor hunting."
