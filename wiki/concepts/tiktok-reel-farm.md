# TikTok Reel Farm

*A cohort of cloud-created TikTok accounts, each posting AI-generated slideshows in viral formats with the operator's product mentioned inline, run as an aggregate impressions-and-CPM play rather than a single-account brand channel.*

## Overview

The third of [[entities/cody-schneider|Cody Schneider]]'s three [[concepts/ai-media-company-playbook|AI media company]] tactics, and the most distinct in shape from the other two: instead of one owned property (directory site, newsletter), this is a multi-account cohort on a borrowed platform, measured in aggregate impressions and blended CPM rather than list size or click count.

## Key Properties / Characteristics

- Multiple accounts run in parallel ("a cohort of 10 accounts") rather than a single branded channel.
- Accounts are created and operated in the cloud via [[entities/doublespeed-ai|DoubleSpeed AI]] — not manually registered/managed devices.
- Content is AI-generated slideshows (via [[entities/nano-banana|Nano Banana]]) following existing viral formats, not original video production.
- Product mention is embedded inline in the slideshow content itself, not run as a separate paid placement.
- Success metric is aggregate impressions at a blended CPM (~$3), a paid-media framing rather than an organic-reach framing.

## How It Works

1. [[entities/doublespeed-ai|DoubleSpeed AI]] creates TikTok accounts in the cloud.
2. [[entities/nano-banana|Nano Banana]] generates slideshow images that follow observed viral formats.
3. The operator's product is mentioned within the slideshow content.
4. Post across the account cohort; measure aggregate impressions and effective CPM.

## Contradictions & Open Questions

- "$3 CPM" implies a paid-media cost framing, but the pipeline as described (organic posting via cloud-created accounts) doesn't mention ad spend — unclear whether this is an effective/imputed CPM or actual spend.
- No discussion of TikTok's platform policies on inauthentic or automated account networks, or account-ban/shadowban risk — a material omission for a strategy explicitly built on cloud-created accounts at scale.
- "Following viral formats" is undefined — no detail on how format selection or performance feedback works.
