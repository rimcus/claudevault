# Content Outbound Flywheel

A LinkedIn GTM system in which content and outbound work as a single reinforcing loop rather than separate motions. Developed by [[Bill Stathopoulos]] / [[SalesCaptain]]. The core thesis: neither content nor outbound alone produces the results they produce together.

## The Two Buyer Journeys

**Journey 1 — Outbound → Content → Conversion:**
1. Email outbound sent to cold prospect
2. Auto-connect request sent (they're now in your network)
3. They see your LinkedIn content while evaluating your offer
4. Engagement flags them as high-priority in your system
5. Conversion sequence triggered

**Journey 2 — Content → Outbound → Sales Call:**
1. Prospect sees your content (via LinkedIn's persona-based algorithm)
2. They follow you or your company
3. They engage with a subsequent post
4. Engagement triggers automated outbound sequence ([[Trigify]] → [[Clay]] → [[HeyReach]])
5. Sales call booked

In both journeys, the content layer pre-warms the prospect before any explicit sales conversation. The outbound layer captures intent signals and acts on them.

## Why the Flywheel Compounds

Each motion generates data that improves the other:
- Content performance reveals which topics resonate → informs outreach messaging
- Outreach engagement reveals which ICPs respond → informs content targeting
- Inbound leads attracted by content have 79% ICP fit (SalesCaptain's reported stat) — because the content attracts buyers who self-select

## The 2026 Algorithm Context

LinkedIn's 2026 algorithm change (persona-based, not network-based) amplified the flywheel's content leg: content now reaches prospects who've never heard of you, as long as it matches their interest persona. This makes the content layer more powerful as a cold-audience acquisition mechanism than it was previously.

## Content Mix (90:20 Rule)

- 40% TOF (reach) — thought leadership, trends, storytelling
- 40% MOF (trust) — demos, playbooks, how-tos
- 20% BOF (conversion) — case studies, results, testimonials

**90:20 Rule:** 90% of LinkedIn growth comes from 20% of posts. Find outliers (2x+ normal reach in past 90 days), create 3 variations, double down.

## Signal Rule (Critical)

The signal informs targeting — never the message. Never say "I saw you liked my post" or "I noticed you attended [Event]." Use the signal to decide *who* to reach out to; use the topic as natural context in copy.
