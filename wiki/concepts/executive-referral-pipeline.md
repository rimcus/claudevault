# Executive & Investor Referral Pipeline

*Turn a company's investor and executive networks into a systematic pipeline source — a fully pre-packaged, low-friction ask to VC portfolio-ops teams, and an internal program that ghostwrites intro messages so executives only have to click send.*

## Overview

Built by [[entities/armand-farrokh|Armand Farrokh]] at [[entities/pave|Pave]], where he joined as employee #9 with effectively zero internal HR-buyer network (Pave's actual customer base) but strong access to the company's CEO and investor relationships. Rather than treating referrals as an occasional, opportunistic add-on to SDR pipeline, this treats them as a managed, cadenced program with its own process.

## Key Properties / Characteristics

- The ask to external partners (VC portfolio-ops teams) is fully pre-packaged — scoring, prioritization, and even draft copy are done in advance, minimizing the partner's own effort to say yes.
- A fixed cadence (biweekly reviews) keeps the program from going stale or one-off.
- Every referral is treated as high-value regardless of outcome — gifted, and closed out rather than left open.
- The internal executive-network variant removes the executive's workload almost entirely (SDRs do the research and drafting; the executive only approves and sends).

## How It Works

**VC portfolio-operations referral program.** Farrokh approached portfolio-ops teams at Pave's investors (Andreessen Horowitz, Index, and Y Combinator named specifically) with a specific, fully-prepared ask: "I've scored your 250 portfolio companies, identified the top 50 fits, and here are the two specific people-leader contacts I need an intro to at each, with a tailored blurb already written for each" — then requested working through 5-10 intros per week on an ongoing basis. He held biweekly review meetings with each VC team to track progress against that cadence, sent gifts to both the referring party and the newly introduced prospect (treating every referral "like gold"), and made sure every intro was actively closed out rather than left to go cold.

**Internal "Exec Shark Tank Week."** A separate, internally-run program: map every first-degree LinkedIn connection of the company's most senior, best-networked executives (at Pave: the CEO, CTO, General Counsel, and Chief People Officer), have SDRs clean up the resulting spreadsheets and ghostwrite ready-to-send introduction messages written in each executive's own voice, then have the executives simply review and click send. This generated 10-20 high-value executive introductions per month from networks that would otherwise have gone almost entirely untapped, since senior executives rarely have the bandwidth to mine and message their own network at that volume unassisted.

## Contradictions & Open Questions

- Neither program's reported metric (intros/week, intros/month) is connected to a downstream pipeline or revenue number in the source — treat these as top-of-funnel volume claims, not proof of ROI.
- Open question: how much of this program's success depends on Pave's specific investor relationships (top-tier VCs with genuinely large, relevant portfolios) versus being replicable by a company with less prominent investors?
