# SDR Org Turnaround Playbook (Flip 2, Fire 2, Hire 2)

*SDR orgs are fast to diagnose and turn around — about 3 weeks, versus 6-12 months for enterprise sales — because the meetings-booked-and-liked-by-AEs feedback loop is nearly immediate. Most underperforming SDR orgs are fixed by three levers: the right people, a raised standard/culture, and minimum-viable modern frameworks.*

## Overview

Documented by [[entities/armand-farrokh|Armand Farrokh]] from his experience taking over a 20-person SDR org (split across San Francisco and Salt Lake City) at [[entities/carta|Carta]] immediately after the team's top performers had been promoted out, leaving a mediocre middle, some underperformers, and some unproven "young guns." His framing is explicitly diagnostic-speed-first: unlike enterprise sales, where a leadership decision might not prove right or wrong for 6-12 months of deal cycles, an SDR org tells you almost immediately whether the right people are calling the right accounts and whether AEs actually like the resulting meetings.

## Key Properties / Characteristics

- Diagnosis speed is the structural advantage of SDR orgs relative to enterprise sales orgs — the same is not true further up the funnel.
- Three general-purpose fix levers, in order of typical impact: right people in the room; raised standard of expectations plus a culture built around it; minimum-viable, modern frameworks (explicitly contrasted against outdated "hustle guru" tactics, name-checking Grant Cardone as the example to avoid).
- The "Flip 2, Fire 2, Hire 2" formula is a specific, three-part personnel-triage move applied within that first lever.

## How It Works

**Flip 2**: identify team members without entrenched bad habits (Farrokh's example: reps who'd never made a single call over 100 dials) — "young guns" with unproven but uncorrupted potential — and personally embed with them against a new, explicit standard (his example: 200 dials/week minimum), setting the cultural tone quickly by being visibly in the trenches alongside them rather than just issuing the mandate from above.

**Fire 2**: remove people showing ongoing underperformance or active resistance to the new culture — explicitly not a snap decision on people who were previously performing, but a fast resolution of *already-identified*, ongoing problems that a new leader might otherwise be tempted to let ride to avoid conflict in their first weeks. See [[concepts/sdr-culture-building]] for the reasoning behind acting fast here.

**Hire 2**: backfill immediately with new hires selected via a redesigned interview process (see [[concepts/sdr-hiring-signal]]), rather than leaving the team understaffed while a slower search runs.

## Contradictions & Open Questions

- No detail on how this formula scales to a much larger or much smaller starting team than 20 — the "2/2/2" framing may be a specific-to-that-team coincidence rather than a fixed ratio.
- Open question: what's the actual base rate of teams where "the right people" genuinely isn't the bottleneck, and the org's problem is purely process/culture? The source presents personnel triage as step one in every case.
