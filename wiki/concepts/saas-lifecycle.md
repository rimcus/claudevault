# SaaS Lifecycle

The complete sequence of phases from initial idea to exit for a software-as-a-service business. [[Hridoy Rehman]]'s 2026 blueprint maps 16 phases with ~85 discrete sub-disciplines, providing a comprehensive skeleton of what "building a SaaS" actually entails.

## Overview

Most SaaS founders operate on an implicit, incomplete model of the lifecycle — strong on the phases they're comfortable with (usually Development and Launch), weak on the rest. Making the full model explicit serves two purposes: as a **planning tool** (what to build toward) and an **audit tool** (what's currently missing).

The blueprint is most useful not as a sequential checklist but as a map: you are always in multiple phases simultaneously, and the diagram shows what you're neglecting.

## The 16 Phases

### Pre-Product
1. **Idea** — Problem Discovery, Market Research, Niche Selection, Competitor Analysis, Opportunity Mapping
2. **Validation** — Customer Interviews, Landing Page Test, Waitlist, Pre-Sales, Demand Testing
3. **Planning** — Product Roadmap, Feature Prioritization, MVP Scope, Tech Stack, Development Plan

### Building
4. **Design** — Wireframes, UI Design, UX Flows, Prototype, Design System
5. **Development** — Frontend, Backend, APIs, Database, Authentication, Integrations
6. **Infrastructure** — Cloud Hosting, DevOps, CI/CD, Monitoring, Security
7. **Testing** — Unit Testing, Integration Testing, Bug Fixing, Performance Testing, Beta Testing

### Go-To-Market
8. **Launch** — Landing Page, Product Hunt, Beta Users, Early Adopters, Public Release
9. **Acquisition** — SEO, Content Marketing, Social Media, Cold Email, Influencer Outreach, Affiliate Marketing
10. **Distribution** — Directories, SaaS Marketplaces, Communities, Partnerships, Integrations

### Revenue & Growth
11. **Conversion** — Sales Funnel, Free Trial, Freemium, Pricing Strategy, Checkout Optimization
12. **Revenue** — Subscriptions, Upsells, Add-ons, Annual Plans, Enterprise Deals
13. **Analytics** — User Tracking, Funnel Analysis, Cohort Analysis, KPI Dashboard, A/B Testing
14. **Retention** — User Onboarding, Email Automation, Customer Support, Feature Adoption, Churn Reduction
15. **Growth** — Referral Programs, Community Building, Product-Led Growth, Viral Loops, Expansion Strategy
16. **Scaling** — Automation, Hiring, Systems, Global Expansion, Exit Strategy

## Key Insight: Distribution is the Most Underrated Phase

Distribution was notably absent from the original blueprint and only added after community pushback. This mirrors a well-documented founder failure mode: technical founders over-index on Development and under-invest in Distribution. The blueprint's omission — and correction — makes this pattern explicit.

## Connection to AI Agents

[[Cody Schneider]]'s [[GTM Agents]] stack automates phases 9 (Acquisition) and 14 (Retention) almost entirely. The @botanium community observation: "assign each part to an agent and start cooking" — the blueprint doubles as an agent assignment map for the full SaaS stack.
