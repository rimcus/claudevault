I used to spend 5 hours every Saturday topping off 15 cold email campaigns with fresh contacts.

Last weekend I used Claude Code to knock it out in two hours with better output.

Here's how:

![Image](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HGHLGkTagAAjwhy?format=jpg&name=large)

---

**Nick Abraham** @NickAbraham12 [2026-04-17](https://x.com/NickAbraham12/status/2045139027142070641)

When you're managing 15+ campaigns, each one needs fresh contacts constantly. That means thousands of data points across thousands of contacts.

Except you can't QA every list at the level of detail you should. So, things slip through.

---

**Nick Abraham** @NickAbraham12 [2026-04-17](https://x.com/NickAbraham12/status/2045139035966890376)

So, we used Discolike's MCP to connect to Claude and our DB.

Instead of pulling lists manually, Claude handles the account search and does QA on the fly.

![Image](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HGHLHXJW4AA31Co?format=jpg&name=large)

---

**Nick Abraham** @NickAbraham12 [2026-04-17](https://x.com/NickAbraham12/status/2045139042296008724)

Importantly, if the accuracy of a search looks low relative to the ICP, it flags it immediately. It says which filters to remove or add as well.

---

**Nick Abraham** @NickAbraham12 [2026-04-17](https://x.com/NickAbraham12/status/2045139044762280123)

It also gets smart about org hierarchy. Say you're selling an SEO tool and your ICP is an SEO Director.

At a smaller company, that title might not exist.

So...

---

**Nick Abraham** @NickAbraham12 [2026-04-17](https://x.com/NickAbraham12/status/2045139047778009317)

Claude looks at the org structure, identifies who that responsibility would fall under, and pulls that person instead.

You get larger lists without sacrificing accuracy.

---

**Nick Abraham** @NickAbraham12 [2026-04-17](https://x.com/NickAbraham12/status/2045139055365476402)

We trained it to catch industry-specific quirks too.

If you're emailing into CRM agencies, a lot of the smaller ones don't list their founders as "owner" or "founder." They list themselves as consultants.

Claude notices that pattern and suggests adding those titles so you're

![Image](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HGHLIalXgAE8u5i?format=jpg&name=large)

---

**Nick Abraham** @NickAbraham12 [2026-04-17](https://x.com/NickAbraham12/status/2045139058326634909)

Once the list is clean, Claude pushes everything straight into Airtable so it's ready to be processed and added into campaigns.

We go from account search to QA to list finalization to campaign-ready in one flow.
