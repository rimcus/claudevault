# Psychology

## 📜 The Rule

<aside>

Your lead decides whether to read or delete **in under 3 seconds.**

They're asking two questions:

1. **"Is this relevant to me?"** → If no, rewrite
2. **"What's in it for me?"** → If nothing, rewrite
</aside>

## 💫 Triggers

<aside>

| Trigger | Why It Works | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Recognition** | "They actually know my situation" | Referencing their specific tech stack |
| **Curiosity** | "I need to know more" | Teasing a result without explaining how |
| **Reciprocity** | "They gave me something first" | Sending value before asking for time |
| **Social Proof** | "Others like me got results" | Mentioning a similar company's outcome |
| **Low Friction** | "This is easy to respond to" | Yes/no questions, no calendar asks |
</aside>
