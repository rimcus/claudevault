# Openers

### 📜 The "Them First" Rule

<aside>

Mention your lead’s company before your own. 

It’s always about them, never about you. 

If you talk about yourself first, you've already lost.

</aside>

### 📄 Templates

<aside>

| Type | Template | When to Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Trigger Event** | "Saw {{company}} just [event]. Congrats!" | Funding, launch, hiring |
| **Observation** | "Noticed your team is [observation]" | Tech stack, strategy |
| **Content Reference** | "Your post on [topic] hit home" | When they're active on LI |
| **Mutual Connection** | "{{mutual}} mentioned you're working on [X]" | Warm intro path |
| **Pattern Interrupt** | "Not sure if this is relevant, but..." | When being direct feels too aggressive |
</aside>

### ✅ Examples (good)

<aside>

> ✅ "Noticed you're using HubSpot but no enrichment tool - curious if that's intentional"
✅ "Your CEO's post on the Series A challenges was spot on, regarding that…"
✅ "Saw you just promoted 2 SDRs to AEs - scaling fast"
> 
</aside>

### ❌ Examples (bad)

<aside>

> ❌ "Hope you're doing well"
❌ "My name is X and help Y"
❌ "I came across your profile and thought we should connect"
> 
</aside>
