# The Structure

<aside>

Every high-performing cold email we wrote in 2025 year followed the same structure.

We booked 90 calls in the first 20 days of December alone with this formula.

</aside>

## 🧑‍🍳 The Recipe

<aside>

**Line 1**: Specific observation about THEM

**Line 2**: Problem they likely face

**Line 2-3**: Your offer/value prop

**Line 4**: Soft CTA (easy to say yes to)

</aside>

## 📜 The Structure Breakdown

<aside>

| Element | Purpose | Word Count |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Opener** | Prove relevance in first 5 words | 5-15 words |
| **Pain/Context** | Show you understand their world | 15-25 words |
| **Value** | What you can do for them | 10-20 words |
| **CTA** | Make it stupid easy to respond | 5-15 words |
</aside>
