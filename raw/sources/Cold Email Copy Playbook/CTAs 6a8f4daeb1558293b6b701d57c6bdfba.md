# CTAs

### ❌ The Problem With "Book a Call"

<aside>

Asking for a 30-minute call in your first message is like proposing marriage on the first date. It just ain’t gonna work.

![image.png](CTAs/image.png)

</aside>

### 📜 The Rule

<aside>
💡

**One email = one CTA**.

Don’t ask if you can send a free resource and a case study and for them to book a call all in one email.

Multiple options create decision fatigue. Keep it simple.

One call to action per email.

</aside>

### 🧪 The Friction Test

<aside>

| CTA Type | Friction Level | Reply Rate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Yes/No question (e.g. “Interested?” | 🟢 Low | Highest |
| "Reply with “yes” and I’ll send you the [free stuff]" | 🟢 Low | High |
| "Mind if I send X?" | 🟡 Medium | Medium |
| "Can I share a quick video?" | 🟡 Medium | Medium |
| "15 mins this week?" | 🔴 High | Low |
| "Book a call here" | 🔴 High | Lowest |
</aside>

### 📄 Examples

<aside>

- "Interested?"
- "Want me to send it?"
- "Make sense to share more?"
- "Want to see it?"
- "Mind if I send over the breakdown?"
- "Want me to shoot over the system?"
- "Cool if I share how they did it?"
- "Would it be crazy to..."
- "Would you hate me if I sent you the [free stuff]?"
</aside>
