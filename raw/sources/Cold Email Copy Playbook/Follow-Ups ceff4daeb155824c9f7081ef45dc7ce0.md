# Follow-Ups

## 📄 Follow-Up Angles

<aside>

### 2️⃣ **Email 2 - The Insight Add:**

> "One more thing - just ran the numbers on a similar campaign. The single biggest lever was [specific insight]. Lifted replies from 3% to 11%. Happy to share the breakdown."
> 

### 3️⃣ **Email 3 - The Social Proof:**

> "Quick example - [similar company] was dealing with the same thing. Went from [before] to [after] in [timeframe]. Think this could work for {{company}}?"
> 

### 4️⃣ **Email 4 - The Free Resource:**

> "Figured I'd send this regardless of timing. Here's [resource] - the exact process [similar company] used. No call required. If you use it and want to chat, I'm around."
> 

### 5️⃣ **Email 5 - The Permission Close:**

> "{{firstName}}, totally fine if now's not the right time. Should I close this out, or worth revisiting in a few months?"
> 
</aside>

## ❌ What NOT to Do

<aside>

- ❌ "Just bumping this up"
- ❌ "Circling back on my last email"
- ❌ "Haven't heard from you"
- ❌ "I'm sure you're busy but..."
</aside>
