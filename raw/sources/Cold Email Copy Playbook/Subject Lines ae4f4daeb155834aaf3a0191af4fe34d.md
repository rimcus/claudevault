# Subject Lines

### 📊 2025 Data

<aside>

| Type | Avg Open Rate |
| --- | --- |
| Question + their company | 38% |
| Lowercase, casual | 34% |
| 2-5 words | 32% |
| Title Case, formal | 24% |
| 7+ words | 19% |

⚠️ Never *actually* track open rates in 2025 or 2026. This will kill your deliverability.

</aside>

### ❌ What to Avoid

<aside>

- ❌ ALL CAPS or excessive punctuation!!!
- ❌ "Partnership opportunity" (screams sales email)
- ❌ False urgency ("URGENT: Read immediately")
- ❌ Emojis in subject lines (screams spam)
- ❌ Your company name (they don't know you yet)
</aside>
