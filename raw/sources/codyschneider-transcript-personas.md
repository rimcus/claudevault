[Cody Schneider](https://x.com/codyschneider)[@codyschneider](https://x.com/codyschneider)

man the biggest arbitrage right now for facebook ads is building personas based on your sales call transcripts what is a persona a persona is a motivation, the primary desire, the core fear beneath it, and the belief someone holds about themselves and your category before you ever reach them a persona is not a demographic, a vertical, a job title, a funnel stage, or the setting of the ad. Those tell you who's in the room and what it looks like, never why anyone acts the transcripts are where all of that already lives, you don't have to guess the fear. someone said it out loud on a call three weeks ago. they told your AE the real reason they were looking, the thing that kept them up, the version of themselves they're trying to become by buying. it's sitting in a recording nobody mined everyone else is targeting "VP of marketing, 50-200 employees, SaaS." that's the room. it tells you nothing about why the VP picks up the phone. so they write an ad to a job title and wonder why it converts at agency-average you write the ad to the motivation. the desire goes in the hook, the fear goes in the body, the belief they already hold becomes the thing you agree with before you reframe it. the demographic just becomes the targeting layer underneath, not the message this is just good advertising nothing has changed, just the tools today mine ten transcripts, pull the exact language, and you'll find three or four motivations that repeat those are your personas
